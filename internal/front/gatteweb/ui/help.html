Help

Commands

mcp-gateway serveRun the gateway.
mcp-gateway upstreamRegister, list, update or deregister a backend.
mcp-gateway toolList, review, approve, revoke or clear tools.
mcp-gateway signApprove a backend's configuration.
mcp-gateway auditRead or verify the audit trail.
mcp-gateway quotaShow the limits and what each analyst has spent.
mcp-gateway accessBlock or unblock one analyst, or list who is blocked.
mcp-gateway maintenanceAnnounce maintenance of the whole gateway, or list it.
mcp-gateway reloadApply roles, groups and quota to the running gateway.
mcp-gateway adminServe the management API for the consoles.
mcp-gateway uiThis console. Add -manage-users (as root) to edit people and roles.
mcp-gateway tuiThe same console in a terminal. Press ? there for its keys.
mcp-gateway checkCheck the configuration and files offline.
mcp-gateway backupCopy the database while serve runs.
mcp-gateway restoreReplace the database with a backup, serve stopped.
mcp-gateway versionPrint the build version.

Words

Config approved
An operator signed the backend's configuration. Says nothing about its tools.
Changed since approved
The configuration changed after it was signed. Not served until approved again.
Tool changed
A tool's definition changed after approval. Not served until reviewed again.
Safe tool
Only reads. Served once approved, to roles that grant it.
Sensitive tool
Can act. Served once approved and cleared, to a non_read role that names it.
non_read
A role that may hold sensitive tools. Turned on by itself, never by a grant.
Clear
Serves an approved sensitive tool. Approval alone does not.
Disable
Stops new sign-ins. A session already open goes on: offboard to cut it off.
Roles file
Where role edits are written. Not applied until you reload.
Reload
Applies the roles file: Reload configuration on the Servers tab, or mcp-gateway reload.
